parent-workspace documents, credentials and unverified CV history are
excluded. Public contact currently uses LinkedIn; no domain mailbox or
personal email is asserted. Blue is default; Glacier is selectable.

artifact upload -> github-pages deployment. It runs on pushes to main or
manual dispatch. Official actions are pinned to checked commit SHAs.
