SOFT GUIDE — SELECTED IDENTITY

Two themes remain: Blue / Light (default) and Glacier / Dark.

Blue / Light
Background #F3F5FA; ink #172B4A; accent #2455C7; pale blue #DCE7FF.

Glacier / Dark
Background #101722; text #EDF2FA; ice-blue accent #91C7F2.

The header Light / Dark control changes the theme instantly and remembers
the visitor's choice. First visits default to light. Other exploratory
palettes have been removed from the current implementation.

The selected Layers identity uses a rhombus above two open layers,
representing infrastructure architecture and the relationship between
platform layers. The original direction-arrow sign has been replaced.

layers-blue.svg and layers-glacier.svg are the active marks, also used for
the favicon. The distinct filenames avoid reusing cached arrow assets.
mark.svg and wordmark.svg provide the light identity; wordmark-glacier.svg
provides the dark identity. social.svg uses the Layers motif. All are
original vector assets; no third-party icon or image was copied.
System Arial/Helvetica, Georgia italic and Consolas/Courier; no remote fonts.
